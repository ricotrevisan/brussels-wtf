# Reader test kit

For [Validate multilingual explanations with representative readers](https://github.com/RicoTrevisan/brussels-wtf/issues/72). Agent review does not count as a reader test.

**Who:** five people, 20 minutes each. Include at least one French reader, one Dutch reader and one English reader, plus one person who follows local politics and one who does not.

**How:** a video call or sitting together, sharing their phone screen. Let them read in their own language. Do not explain the site, and do not help unless they are stuck for two minutes. Note what they say.

## Pages

Pick by the reader's commune once it is live. Until then:

- Long proposal page: https://brussels.wtf/meetings/ixelles/270
- Page with documented decisions: https://brussels.wtf/meetings/forest/2026-03-24
- Homepage: https://brussels.wtf/

## Tasks

1. **First look (homepage).** "What is this site for? What would you do first?"
2. **Find.** "Find the most recent thing your commune's council discussed."
3. **Scan (long page).** "Which item here would matter most to you? How did you choose it?" Record the time taken and whether they gave up.
4. **Understand.** Pick one item: "In your own words, what is being proposed? Who does it affect? What amount or conditions apply?"
5. **Status.** "Has this been decided? How do you know?" Then ask the same on a documented decision.
6. **Verify.** "Show me where this comes from."
7. **Correct.** "You think this is wrong. What would you do?" Stop before they submit.
8. **Return.** "Would you come back? What would bring you back?"

## Pass line

Tasks 2, 4, 5 and 6 are completed unaided by at least four of five readers. This is a formative check, not statistical proof.

## Record per reader

Language, commune, how closely they follow local politics, pass or fail per task, exact confusing wording, and any language difference.

## Decision to make after five sessions

- **Task 3 fails:** build a top-of-page summary for long meetings under [Explain the actual municipal action](https://github.com/RicoTrevisan/brussels-wtf/issues/62).
- **Task 4 fails:** change the generation prompt before scaling further.
- **Task 8 is a clear no:** revisit the product before adding new sources.

Record the sample, findings and scale or revise decision on the issue, and open one issue per observed failure.
