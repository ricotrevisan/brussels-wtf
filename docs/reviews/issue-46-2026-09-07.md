## Standards

No remaining concrete blockers. The four original findings are addressed:

- Exact role, table scope and reporting period are immutable declaration
  semantics, checked against an inspected source contract and revalidated OCR.
- Complete normalized declarant names replace substring matches. Active identity
  uniqueness and independent minutes corroboration gate public projection,
  including historical eligibility.
- OCR verification runs on the isolated `ocr` queue; source-specific flags are
  recorded in processing provenance. Discovery consumes hash-checked artifacts.
- Missing, ambiguous and uncorroborated identities create actionable source review
  cases. Unknown bytes and invalid layouts fail closed.

Role labels support French, Dutch and English while preserving source-native
wording. Declaration evidence uses `municipal_declaration` / `official_declaration`,
not decision semantics. The inspected scanned-source contract explains why the
native embedded OCR layer cannot satisfy this source's quality policy.

## Spec

No remaining concrete blockers. The five original findings are addressed:

- Public person routes render the exact role, body, dates, physical-page citation,
  and separate dated history under localized “Verified facts” headings.
- Signature acceptance binds to an inspected immutable PDF checksum and selected
  declaration/attestation pages. Finding the word “Signature” alone is insufficient.
- Identity uses exact complete names and independent trusted minutes evidence.
  Later normalized-name collisions withdraw the declaration from public views.
- Inspected corrected revisions explicitly supersede their predecessor, retain
  old evidence, and handle acquisition before predecessor verification. Superseded
  claims remain dated profile history and are excluded from new feed updates.
- SQL dispatch cannot recurse from a declaration into another declaration;
  withdrawing independent evidence removes the dependent public claim.

Acquisition now archives the official index and its exact approved PDF link,
then uses the normal extraction/OCR/fact/verification pipeline. Derived declaration
text is minimized in normal and review-retry paths. Public projections and feeds
exclude financial fields, private identifiers and signature material.

## Source contract and limits

The actual privately acquired 132-page PDF was visually inspected. Physical page
14 binds Gautier CALOMNE to the municipal executive mandate for **December 2024**;
page 17 has the dated signed attestation. The inspected SHA-256 and a minimal
source-derived OCR fixture are documented in
[the fixture contract](../../test/support/fixtures/ixelles/mandates/README.md).

A disposable container with the production PDF packages successfully ran the
actual source through force-OCR with page segmentation mode 6 on pages 14 and 17.
The parser accepted page 14 and dates 2024-12-01 through 2024-12-31. Default redo-OCR
duplicated the embedded text; force-OCR without that segmentation missed headers.
The selected profile is pinned in OCR input/config provenance and verifier reruns.

This is one inspected declaration, not a general declaration parser. New bytes
require a newly inspected contract before publication; automated correction
supersession operates only on those approved revisions. The raw and OCR PDFs are
private evidence, and have not been committed. Integration identities/documents
are synthetic; no real person's production identity or fact was manufactured.
No production publication or deployment was performed.
