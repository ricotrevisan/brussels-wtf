# brussels.wtf

Evidence-backed reporting on municipal council meetings in Ixelles, Uccle, Forest, and the City of Brussels.

The product will publish French, Dutch, and English coverage of upcoming proposals and final decisions while preserving links to authoritative sources. Organizations mentioned in council material will be resolved to legal entities where evidence permits, with sourced ownership, management, and council-member relationships subject to editorial review.

Planning is tracked through a GitHub Wayfinder map before implementation tickets are cut.
